<h1 class="research-page-title">Research</h1>
<p class="editorial-page-lede">We combine in vivo perturbation, single-cell and spatial genomics, stem-cell models, and computational analysis to understand cardiovascular biology.</p>
</header>

<h4 class="research-title">Direct Cardiac Reprogramming for Heart Regeneration</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We investigate how fibroblast identity can be remodeled into induced cardiomyocyte-like cells using compact reprogramming cocktails, with the goal of improving cardiac repair after injury.</p>
</div>

<p class="research-desc">Working with clinical and computational collaborators, we aim to functionally interpret disease-associated genes and variants using CRISPR perturbation, single-cell readouts, iPSC models, and organoid systems.</p>
